# English 6 · Audio

Audio for *English 6 · Grammar, Structure & Reading* (B1+), by Daniel Lara.

Site: <https://lara-montano.github.io/ingles6-audio/> — one page per track (`/<code>/`), opened by the
QR codes printed in the book. The pages are generated by `scripts/sitio_audio.py` in the book's
repository; the audio files go in `audio/<code>.m4a` (and `<code>-slow.m4a`, `<code>.txt`).
